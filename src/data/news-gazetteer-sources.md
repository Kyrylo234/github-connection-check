# News coordinate lookup

- Country names, ISO codes and national centres: https://github.com/mledoze/countries (ODbL 1.0).
- Cities with population at least 100,000: GeoNames cities15000 export, https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/ (CC BY 4.0).
- Retrieved 15 November 2026. Exact alias match within an ISO country only; ambiguous names are not resolved.
- Country centres are approximate geographic references, not claims of precise event positions. No model-generated coordinates are accepted.
